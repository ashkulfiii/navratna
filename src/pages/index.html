Handmade by Navya

Navya dreamed of becoming a jewelry designer when she was five years old, and every piece in this collection is designed and made by her hand.

In fourth grade she sold her handmade polymer clay jewelry and raised $10,000 for the Atlanta Community Food Bank in under two months. Half of every sale still goes to the food bank and other community causes.
