About

A house of handmade adornments.

NavRatna was founded off of creativity, community, and the dream of a five year old girl hoping to be a jewelry designer. Since the day the idea formed in my head, I have been working on my craft.

In 2021, when I was in fourth grade, I started a fundraiser where I sold my handmade polymer clay jewelry and raised $10,000 in less than two months for the Atlanta Community Food Bank.

Every year since then, we have proudly kept that momentum going, raising thousands of dollars for the ACFB during annual fairs. To uphold this tradition of fashion with a purpose, NavRatna commits 50% of all proceeds to the ACFB and other vital community causes.
